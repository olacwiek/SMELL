# SMELL pilot, pair 1

Data from an informal pilot of the word-creation task of the SMELL project (Study 1), recorded in Toruń on 25 November 2026. Two colleagues, native speakers of Polish, agreed on a new two-syllable word for each of five household odors: soap, garlic, fish sauce, a limescale and rust remover (W5, Lidl), and whole nutmeg. They shared one jar per odor and could not see what was inside. No ratings were collected.

words.csv has one row per agreed word. candidates.csv lists all forms that came up during the negotiation and what happened to them. transcript_short.csv is a shortened transcript of the instructions and the five negotiations in Polish, with an English translation.

Times are in seconds from the start of the recording. s_to_agreement is the time from handing over the jar to the moment the word was accepted. In route, blend marks words combined from parts of existing words and sound marks words built from sound. transcript_rule shows how the exclusion rule planned for Study 1 would treat each word: words built from content words are excluded, words built from interjections or onomatopoeia are kept and flagged.

In the transcript, E is the experimenter and P1 and P2 are the two participants. [...] marks omissions. Names and remarks unrelated to the task were removed. The recording was transcribed automatically and checked against the audio. The audio is not shared.
